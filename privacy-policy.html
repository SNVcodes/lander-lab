Lander Lab — Privacy Policy

Effective 3 October 2026

Who we are

Lander Lab ("the game") is made by SNVcodes ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is involved when you play, who handles it, and the choices you have. Questions: tekscope333@gmail.com.

The short version

You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or location, and we do not run servers that collect information about you.

Your progress — levels flown, credits, ships, settings and what you have bought — is saved only on your device.

Two Google services are part of the game: Google Play, which handles purchases, and Google AdMob, which shows ads to players who have not bought Remove Ads. Those services collect some information under Google's own privacy policies, described below.

What is stored on your device

Game progress and settings, and a record of purchases you have made, are stored in the game's private storage on your device. This information is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data, and progress can be reset from the game's Settings. Purchases of Remove Ads, Sandbox Pro and the Pilot's Bundle can be restored from Google Play after a reinstall; credits are part of the saved progress on the device.

Purchases — Google Play

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google provides the game with a record of the purchase (which item, a purchase token and order details) so it can be delivered. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising — Google AdMob

Unless you have bought Remove Ads, the game may show a full-screen ad between flights. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

Where the law requires it — including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent message, and you can change your choice from the game's Settings. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Permissions

The game uses internet access (for ads and purchases), Google Play's billing permission, and the advertising ID permission used by AdMob. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, files or precise location.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us at tekscope333@gmail.com and we will help have it removed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to its use. Because we do not hold personal information about you on our own systems, most requests concern Google's services; you can make them through your Google Account, and we will help if you write to tekscope333@gmail.com.

Security and transfers

Information handled by Google may be processed in countries other than your own, under Google's safeguards. Data on your device is protected by your device's own security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.

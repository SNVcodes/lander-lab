Twinburner — Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Who we are

Twinburner: Lander Simulator ("the game") is made by SNVcodes ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is involved when you play, who handles it, and the choices you have. Questions: tekscope333@gmail.com.

The short version

You do not need an account. We do not ask for your name, contacts, photos or location, and we do not run servers that collect information about you.

Your progress — levels flown, story progress, credits, ships, settings and what you have bought — is saved only on your device.

Google services are part of the game: Google Play, which handles purchases, ratings and updates, and Google AdMob, which shows ads. Those services collect some information under Google's own privacy policies, described below.

If you choose to write to us — with SEND FEEDBACK in the game, or by email — we receive your email address and whatever you send.

What is stored on your device

Game progress and settings, and a record of purchases you have made, are stored in the game's private storage on your device. This information is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the game or clear its data, and progress can be reset from the game's Settings. Purchases of Remove Ads, Sandbox Pro and the Pilot's Bundle can be restored from Google Play after a reinstall; credits are part of the saved progress on the device.

Purchases — Google Play

In-app purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. Google provides the game with a record of the purchase (which item, a purchase token and order details) so it can be delivered. Google's handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising — Google AdMob

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Unless you have bought Remove Ads, the game shows small banner ads on some menus and results screens, a full-screen ad now and then between flights, and occasionally one when you come back to the game after a while. Some rewards are offered for watching an ad — reviving after a crash, doubling a reward, free credits, or trying an item — and these play only when you choose them. Remove Ads removes every ad except the ones you choose to watch for a reward.

To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. On recent versions of Android, AdMob may also use Android's own advertising services (such as Topics and attribution reporting), which you can manage in your device's settings.

Where the law requires it — including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent message, and you can change your choice at any time under Settings → About & Legal → AD PRIVACY CHOICES. Wherever you are, you can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Feedback, ratings and updates

SEND FEEDBACK opens your own email app with a message to us. If you send it, we receive your email address and what you wrote, and use them only to reply to you and to improve the game. We do not add you to any mailing list or share your email with anyone. Ask us at tekscope333@gmail.com and we will delete your messages.

Now and then the game may ask whether you would like to rate it. Ratings and reviews are made through Google Play, under Google's terms; we only see what Google Play shows publicly or in its developer tools. The game may also check Google Play for a newer version and offer to update.

Permissions

The game uses internet and network-state access (for ads, purchases and updates), Google Play's billing permission, the advertising ID permission and Android's advertising services permissions used by AdMob, and background-work permissions that Google's libraries use to finish their tasks. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us at tekscope333@gmail.com and we will help have it removed.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in Europe, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to its use. Apart from any messages you send us, we do not hold personal information about you; most requests concern Google's services, which you can make through your Google Account, and we will help if you write to tekscope333@gmail.com.

Security and transfers

Information handled by Google may be processed in countries other than your own, under Google's safeguards. Data on your device is protected by your device's own security.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the same address with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the game's update notes.

Lander Lab

Two engines, a lot of gravity, and the ground. Lander Lab is a physics flight game about setting a craft down gently — across storms, minefields, meteor showers, gun lines and worlds that do not forgive.

Made by

Sound

Sound effects come from Pixabay (https://pixabay.com), used under the Pixabay Content License, which allows free use. A heartfelt thank-you to Pixabay and to the creators who share their work there so generously. Some ambient sounds were made for the game.

Artwork

All artwork in Lander Lab was created for the game by SNVcodes, with the help of AI image-generation tools, and is part of the game.

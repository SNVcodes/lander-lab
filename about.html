Twinburner

Two engines, a lot of gravity, and the ground. Twinburner is a lander simulator: a physics flight game about setting a craft down gently — across storms, minefields, meteor showers, gun lines and worlds that do not forgive.

Learn to fly in Flight School, then take on the Missions — more than a hundred landings across sixteen sectors — or go down into Kharon Deep, a hundred hand-made levels inside an ancient structure, where Vega, your mission control, guides you through the story of the lost ship Meridian and the fifty people who went down after its signal. The Practice Range, Contracts and a Sandbox are there when you want to fly for its own sake.

Artwork

All artwork in Twinburner, including the story pictures of Kharon Deep, was created for the game by SNVcodes, with the help of AI image-generation tools, and is part of the game.

Voices

Vega and the other voices on the radio were made for the game with the Kokoro text-to-speech model (Apache 2.0). Thank you to the people who build and share it.
